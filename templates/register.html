WHERE IT ALL STARTED

Garden Compound, Lusaka • 1997

1997 - The Beginning

Prophetess Elfridah Mhusambazi started in Garden Compound with 150 orphans. She collected them, enrolled them at Achievers Christian Academy Grade 1-7, gave them lunch Monday to Friday, and gave them a chance to sing and testify at conferences.

Skills That Changed Lives

Women were trained in Tie & Dye, Flower Gardening, Food Covers and Doormats. Certificates were given. Women started businesses and fed their families.

Today - 4000 Women Strong

From 150 orphans to 4000 Women Strong. Monthly meetings First Saturday 13:00-18:00 at Mass Media, Lusaka. Over 10,000 women reached in Zambia + 500 abroad. Goal: 100,000 in next 10 years.
