ORGANISATION PROFILE (From Official Document)
Women of Influence International is a Zambian registered organization under PACRA with Registration Number 51848. Women of Influence Ministry is a Para Church Organization which has no plan whatsoever of becoming a church, because we are already affiliated to Barak Ministries, which is our mother body and stands as our covering.

Founded in 1997, reaching out to women, widows and orphans in preaching the word and supporting through empowerment trainings. For past 23 years we have reached over 10,000 women over Zambia, and over 500 in America, Europe, South Africa, Botswana and Tanzania. We believe we can reach 100,000 in next 10 years.

VISION

To influence and empower women, widows and orphans in various aspects of their life through skills training; and to help realize and bring out the potential in every individual person we come in contact with according to their developmental need.

MISSION

  • To raise Women to a place of Influence in Life, Family, Community, Working Places, Society and help them realize their calling.
  • To provide Ministry of the Word through Conferences, Seminars, Books and Tapes.
  • To help network Pastors and Pastors Wives Conference.
  • To provide Health Facilities, Education for Orphans, Counseling of HIV/AIDS and Empowering Widows and OVC Care Givers with life skills.

OBJECTIVES (5 Pillars)

  • 1. To preach and teach the word of God and train women to be prayer warriors in their lives and families.
  • 2. To offer counselling and support to the oppressed and those affected with HIV & AIDS.
  • 3. To raise orphans and help give them an education through to university level so that they can have a meaningful life.
  • 4. To raise widows, pastors' wives and the vulnerable so as to empower them with skills training and teach them how to manage their business.
  • 5. To help disadvantaged groups identify their callings and purposes in life.

ACTIVITIES OF WOI

  • Preaching of the gospel - taking people out of darkness into light. Colossians 1:12-14
  • Teaching women how to pray and see results. Esther 4:8; 14-17 (Hosea 4:6)
  • Encouraging talent & creativity to make business that will empower financially.
  • Mobilize orphans from community and send them to school up to university + empowerment skills.
  • Widows identified and empowered with skill trainings and creativity.
  • Less privileged children helped to complete education.
  • Counselling and support in various issues including HIV & AIDS.
