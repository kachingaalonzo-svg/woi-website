ORGANISATION PROFILE - OFFICIAL (From Your DOCX)

Women of Influence International is Zambian registered under PACRA Registration Number 51848. Para Church Organization affiliated to Barak Ministries (mother body covering).

Founded 1997, reaching women, widows and orphans through word of God and empowerment trainings. Over 10,000 women Zambia + 500 in America, Europe, South Africa, Botswana, Tanzania. Goal: 100,000 in next 10 years.

VISION

To influence and empower women, widows and orphans in various aspects of life through skills training; and to help realize and bring out potential in every person according to developmental need.

MISSION

  • Raise Women to place of Influence in Life, Family, Community, Work, Society
  • Ministry of Word through Conferences, Seminars, Books, Tapes
  • Network Pastors and Pastors Wives Conference
  • Health Facilities, Education for Orphans, HIV/AIDS Counseling, Empowering Widows

OBJECTIVES

  • Preach word, train prayer warriors
  • Counselling & support HIV/AIDS & oppressed
  • Raise orphans education to university
  • Raise widows, pastors wives with skills & business
  • Help disadvantaged identify callings

ACTIVITIES

  • Gospel preaching - darkness to light Col 1:12-14
  • Teach prayer - Esther 4:8, Hosea 4:6
  • Talent & creativity for business
  • Mobilize orphans to school + skills
  • Widows empowerment trainings
  • Education for less privileged
  • Counselling HIV/AIDS
