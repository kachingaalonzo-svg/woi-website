Our Mission - Raising a Blessed Generation

WOI was founded in 1997 by Prophetess Elfridah Mhusambazi with a vision to empower women spiritually, socially, and economically. For 29 years, we have gathered every First Saturday to worship, learn, and grow together.

🙏

Spiritual Growth

Monthly teachings, prayer lines, and impartation from Prophetess Elfridah. Follow live on fb.com/woiofficial

👭

Sisterhood

4000 women supporting each other in Lusaka and beyond. A safe space for women of all backgrounds.

📖

Teaching

Practical teachings on family, marriage, business, and raising a blessed generation. Watch on Facebook.

📅

First Saturday

Every First Saturday 13:00-18:00 we gather in Lusaka for 5 hours of worship, word, and fellowship.

Founder - Prophetess Elfridah Mhusambazi

Visionary leader since 1997. 29 years of raising women of influence in Zambia and beyond. Her heart is to see every woman walk in her God-given purpose and raise a blessed generation.
