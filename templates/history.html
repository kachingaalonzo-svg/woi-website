Story From The Book

Raising a Blessed Generation

Her own life is a beautiful story of God's power. God healed her from a terrible sickness that doctors could not cure.

In 1997, Prophetess Elfridah Mhusambazi started WOI with a few women in Lusaka. Today 3000-4000 women gather every first Saturday 13:00-18:00.

This movement is built on the Word — no dress code, free entry, mothers raising blessed children.

Monthly: First Saturday 13:00-18:00 • Free • Founder: Elfridah Mhusambazi • Est. 1997
