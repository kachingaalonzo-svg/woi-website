From the Book: Who She Is

"Prophetess Doctor Elfridah Mhusambazi Faith is a great leader and a loving mother to many Christians in Zambia. She started Barak Ministries International and Women of Influence in Lusaka. For more than twenty-five years she has served God with all her heart. Her own life is a beautiful story of God's power. God healed her from a terrible sickness and this miracle changed her life forever. Because of this she decided to help other people find healing and peace."

This is her testimony as written in the book — shared as a faith story of God's power in her life.

The Garden Compound Story — Private History

Kept here — not on public homepage — as you requested to avoid offense

In Garden Compound, Lusaka, Elfridah found children who were most vulnerable — orphans with no school, no hope.

She took 150 orphans herself.

Step 1: Took them from Garden Compound
Step 2: Started her own school, put each and every one inside — Grade 1 to 9. Uniforms, books, teachers — all through WOI.
Step 3: After Grade 9, empowered them for 3 years — life skills, Word of God, character
Step 4: Transferred them to Northmead School for secondary education

Today, some are in college, some have jobs, some have families. Garden → WOI School → Northmead → Leaders. This is why WOI does not just preach — we build.

Why private? You said keep this off front page to avoid offense — so we keep it here for those who want deep history.
